Open-source plush robot

A small orange bear that turns its head and reaches out to hug or wave.

Evokin is a Mirumi-style companion: a furry shell around a motorised head-pan joint and two forward-facing arms, three Feetech FS90MG servos, and a planned ESP32-S3 brain with a camera and a voice.

How it looks

Blender Cycles renders of the v4 fur model, plus CAD stills of the mechanism. Click for the same frame at this page’s resolution; full-size PNGs are in the repository.

How it works

Three metal-gear micro servos do all of the motion. The shells are FDM-printed and closed on a registration lip. Fail-safe is a requirement, not a finished feature.

Head pan

An FS90MG in the yaw collar turns a coupler blade in a slot in the head. A printed 0.8 mm thrust washer takes the axial load. Software clamps pan to ±42°; the printed hard stop is at about ±47°. No tilt axis — it does not fit cleanly beside the camera.

Forward-facing arms

One FS90MG per shoulder, direct drive, in a pod moulded into the back shell. 0° is straight forward and + is up: software range −55° to +70°, for a hug or a wave. Paw force at stall is about 3.4 N (datasheet, not measured).

Fail-safe

Direct drive has no mechanical breakaway. The plan is to cut the 5 V servo rail when the arms are parked, or when a current sensor sees a stall, so the servos can back-drive and the arms go limp. That trip is not built yet.

Shells and fasteners

Male lip on the back half, female groove on the front, 0.25 mm FDM clearance, 0.40 mm elephant-foot chamfer. One scheme everywhere: M2.5 at 2.8 mm clearance / 2.1 mm pilot. Print the test coupon first.

Electronics

The planned brain is an ESP32-S3, not the Raspberry Pi Zero still drawn as a tray dummy in the CAD. v4 needs three servo PWM channels. Planning notes, GPIO cautions and a bring-up checklist are in electronics/esp32-board-notes.md.

Prototype paths

  • Option A: Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, plus spare WROOM-1U-N16R8 modules. Three spare PWM pins confirmed (e.g. D0–D2).
  • Option B: DFRobot DFR1154. Only GPIO43/44 free, so add a PCA9685 servo driver over I2C.

Custom board target

  • ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8, 65×55 mm, 4-layer.
  • BQ24074, TPS63020, TPS61088 (not MT3608), INA219, TPS259241 eFuse.
  • OV2640/OV3660 on a 24-pin FPC through the neck; INMP441; MAX98357A.
