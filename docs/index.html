Open-source plush robot

A small orange bear that turns its head and clasps its paws.

Evokin is a Mirumi-style companion: a furry shell around a printed yaw joint, a cord-drum arm drive, two Feetech FS90MG servos, and a planned ESP32-S3 brain with a camera and a voice.

How it looks

Blender Cycles renders of the v3 fur model, plus CAD stills of the mechanism. Click for the same frame at this page’s resolution; full-size PNGs are in the repository.

How it works

Two metal-gear micro servos do all of the motion. The shells are FDM-printed and closed on a registration lip. Fail-open is a requirement, not a finished feature.

Head yaw

An FS90MG in the yaw collar turns a coupler blade in a slot in the head. A printed 0.8 mm thrust washer takes the axial load. Software should clamp yaw to about ±42° so the hard stop is not a running face.

Arm clasp

A second FS90MG turns a cord drum 1:1 with the horn. 0–45° of arm close is 6.28 mm of cord, which is 35.12° of servo travel. Soft foam pads are meant to meet in the middle; pad stiffness, and therefore grip force, is unknown.

Fail-open

The plan is to cut the 5 V servo rail when the paws are parked, or when a current sensor sees a child pulling the arms apart, so the servos can back-drive and the paws go limp. A mechanical weak link in the cord has not been designed or rated.

Shells and fasteners

Male lip on the back half, female groove on the front, 0.25 mm FDM clearance, 0.40 mm elephant-foot chamfer. One scheme everywhere: M2.5 at 2.8 mm clearance / 2.1 mm pilot. Print the test coupon first.

Electronics

The planned brain is an ESP32-S3, not the Raspberry Pi Zero still drawn as a tray dummy in the v3 CAD. Planning notes, GPIO cautions and a bring-up checklist are in electronics/esp32-board-notes.md.

Prototype paths

  • Option A: Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, plus spare WROOM-1U-N16R8 modules.
  • Option B: DFRobot DFR1154, if two servo GPIOs are free — still unverified.

Custom board target

  • ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8, 65×55 mm, 4-layer.
  • BQ24074, TPS63020, TPS61088 (not MT3608), INA219, TPS259241 eFuse.
  • OV2640/OV3660 on a 24-pin FPC through the neck; INMP441; MAX98357A.

  • Prototype. v3 CAD is complete and unprinted. Motion, grip force, fail-open and wear are untested.
